Plot

This page is not prose itself. It's a control plane for managing the narrative structure of the Inner Shell universe. Here, I will outline key narrative elements and story arcs. As the story develops, this page will be updated with new details and insights.

Story Arcs

The story will be siloed as two main arcs: the first arc will be a street level, boots on the ground narrative focused on several characters navigating the challenges and unforseen consequences of living in a world dominated by advanced AI and ubiquitous technology. The second and concurrent arc will follow characters who occupy a world of corporate and governmental power, exploring the machinations and ethical dilemmas faced by those in control of the technology shaping society.

The real meaning of "The Inner Shell"

The inner shell is not metaphorical. It is literal, biological, infrastructural, and computational. I-07 is the inner shell.
