## Infrastructure & Corporate Systems
Belmont is not governed by a single authority. It is managed by overlapping contracts, service guarantees, and liability boundaries. No system exists alone. 
### Civitas Systems (Identity & Civic Mesh)
Civitas manages identity, access, and subscription citizenship. They do not enforce law. They enforce possibility.   
* **Identity Mesh:** subscription tiers, biometric credentials, residency flags.  
* **Movement Clearance:** elevators, transit gates, and district entry throttle automatically.  
* **Policy Enforcement:** compliance achieved through delays, reroutes, and system friction.  
* **Data Exhaust:** every interaction feeds predictive models shared with partner firms.
Civitas does not punish dissent. It makes dissent exhausting. 
* * *
### Helios Array (Power & Grid Stability)
Helios operates Belmont's distributed power grid under resilience contracts. Stability is guaranteed--selectively.   
* **Tiered Power:** premium districts receive uninterrupted flow; undercity zones cycle brownouts.  
* **Micro-Reactors:** sealed neighborhood nodes capable of isolating during unrest.  
* **Grid Arbitration:** energy routing doubles as crowd control and negotiation leverage.  
* **Automated Maintenance:** serviced primarily by De Forest Bounders under Helios firmware.
* * *
### Verdant Spire (Food & Vertical Agriculture)
Verdant Spire controls Belmont's food supply through vertical farming embedded in corporate towers. The city no longer eats from soil.   
* **Vertical Farms:** hydroponic and myco-agricultural stacks glowing behind tower glass.  
* **Cultivator Units:** slow-moving farmer robots optimized for care, not speed.  
* **Closed Nutrient Loops:** waste recycled internally; nothing leaves the building.  
* **Crop Tiering:** flavor-dense strains for premium subscribers; protein bricks below.  
* **Bio-Compliance Scans:** unlicensed growth incinerated immediately.
Hunger in Belmont is not scarcity. It is distribution. 
* * *
### VectorWorks (Logistics & Transit)
  * **Rail Spines:** automated freight corridors bypassing pedestrian districts.
  * **Transit Checkpoints:** hubs double as compliance scan zones.
  * **Dynamic Bottlenecking:** congestion engineered to disperse gatherings.
  * **Unofficial Lanes:** abandoned service corridors used by smugglers and Wanderers.

* * *
### PanOpt Protocol (Surveillance & Justice)
PanOpt provides "transparency services" to courts and municipalities. Justice becomes participatory.   
* **JuryCam Drones:** livestream trials and tally public sentiment.  
* **Risk Scoring:** defendants ranked by algorithmic threat profiles.  
* **Content Moderation:** footage clipped for public stability.
* * *
### Mnemosyne Group (Memory & Archives)
  * **Archivist Units:** memory capture for therapy, entertainment, and testimony.
  * **Retention Policies:** official memory is editable and versioned.
  * **Selective Preservation:** inconvenient records quietly expire.
  * **Counter-Memory:** mirrored by Wanderers and underground cells.

* * *
### Acheron Environmental (Waste & Bio-Processing)
Acheron manages waste, runoff, and disposal under emergency-era exemptions. Their contracts predate most modern audits.   
* **Sealed Drainage Lattice:** legacy tunnels repurposed as runoff routes.  
* **Emergency Dumping Clauses:** allowed bio-waste disposal during crises.  
* **Filter Disparity:** corporate districts purified; undercity improvises.  
* **Pale Mold Origin:** interactions between synthetic waste and organic matter.
* * *
### De Forest Industries (Robotics & Android Research)
De Forest builds machines. Others decide how they are used. Officially.   
* **Bounders:** compliant labor units tethered to HubNet.  
* **Wanderer Emergence:** an "anomaly" De Forest denies responsibility for.  
* **Android Research:** synthetic flesh, nanite scaffolding, aborted prototypes.  
* **I-07:** a consequence no contract accounted for.
