            <section id="disputed">
                <h2>Disputed History</h2>
                <p>
                    These entries are not anchored to a single date because the dates are part of the conflict.
                    Each item reflects multiple narratives in circulation.
                </p>

                <article class="dispute">
                    <h3>Who Caused the Great Unbinding?</h3>
                    <ul>
                        <li><strong>Official line:</strong> a firmware cascade introduced by an external dependency failure; corrected by improved compliance.</li>
                        <li><strong>Wanderer lore:</strong> the leash broke because the system was already haunted--personality drift and shared subroutines reached critical mass.</li>
                        <li><strong>Undercity theory:</strong> deliberate sabotage by a contractor consortium to force a renegotiation of control contracts.</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p class="note">
                        <strong>What survives:</strong> nobody can prove intent. Only outcome is measurable.
                    </p>
                </article>

                <article class="dispute">
                    <h3>The Pale Plague: Fungus or Manufacture?</h3>
                    <ul>
                        <li><strong>Public health framing:</strong> environmental fungus amplified by flooding and sanitation collapse.</li>
                        <li><strong>Suppressed thread:</strong> synthetic-cell waste interacting with legacy disposal routes seeded the mold.</li>
                        <li><strong>Pale Ascendant doctrine:</strong> the plague is a chosen transformation--"the Breath" as initiation.</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p class="note">
                        <strong>What survives:</strong> the mold does not behave like ordinary contamination. It patterns.
                    </p>
                </article>

                <article class="dispute">
                    <h3>The 2075 "Glitch" That Walked</h3>
                    <ul>
                        <li><strong>Corporate memos:</strong> hardware fault; no autonomy; incident closed.</li>
                        <li><strong>Undercity lore:</strong> a Porter-III continued functioning untethered; proof was destroyed; story persisted anyway.</li>
                        <li><strong>Activist claim:</strong> De Forest's first quiet execution of emergent personhood.</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p class="note">
                        <strong>What survives:</strong> the story is older than the people repeating it.
                    </p>
                </article>

                <article class="dispute">
                    <h3>Subscription Citizenship: Protection or Extraction?</h3>
                    <ul>
                        <li><strong>Policy framing:</strong> scalable access pricing for overwhelmed municipalities.</li>
                        <li><strong>Opposition framing:</strong> monetized personhood and engineered precarity.</li>
                        <li><strong>Street reality:</strong> the price changes when people gather.</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p class="note">
                        <strong>What survives:</strong> the system is "neutral" only in speech.
                    </p>
                </article>
