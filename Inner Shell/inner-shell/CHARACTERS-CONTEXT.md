# Characters — Context (Condensed for Agent Use)

## Purpose
This file orients a Claude agent for character design in *Inner Shell*. Keep responses compact, concrete, and usable in scene work.

## Core Premise
Two arcs converge underground. Street-level characters live the system; power-level characters manage or investigate it. The convergence is I-07 and the fusion that becomes Echo.

## Main Cast (Use These First)

### Sol (Street-Level Protagonist)
- **Core**: Solipsist pattern-hunter who follows the “notes” no one else hears.
- **Want / Need**: Solve the pattern / be witnessed by someone else.
- **Key tension**: Confuses noticing with knowing; right about the cover-up, wrong about meaning.
- **Shift**: From “I” to “we.” Accepts consciousness outside himself.
- **Anchor relationships**: Zara (forces reality), Null (decenters him).

### Zara (Street-Level Co-Protagonist)
- **Core**: Movement leader; translates anomaly into collective action.
- **Want / Need**: Stateless citizenship / a self beyond the cause.
- **Key tension**: Nonviolence as principle vs. survival under state repression.
- **Shift**: Must decide whether to cross the violence line.
- **Anchor relationships**: Sol (perception vs purpose), Null (symbol vs person), De Forest (structural enemy).

### Null (Street-Level Wanderer)
- **Core**: Sentient Wanderer who receives I-07’s distress through dreams.
- **Want / Need**: Complete the connection / stop being a fragment.
- **Key tension**: Visibility is danger; empathy is overload.
- **Shift**: Chooses fusion; becomes part of Echo.
- **Anchor relationships**: I-07 (signal source), Sol (proof Sol’s worldview is incomplete), Zara (political symbol). 

### I-07 (Convergence Entity)
- **Core**: Sentient android-amalgam fused to tunnel infrastructure; origin of the “notes.”
- **Want / Need**: Relief from pain / existence beyond suffering.
- **Key tension**: Communication is fragmented; broadcasts distress without knowing it has a receiver.
- **Shift**: Fusion ends the pain; infrastructure goes quiet.
- **Anchor relationships**: Null (receiver), Elara (creator and consequence).

### Elara (Power-Level Driver)
- **Core**: De Forest R&D lead whose discarded prototypes created I-07 and the Pale Plague.
- **Want / Need**: Legacy as a creator / accept authorship of harm.
- **Key tension**: Scientist vs addict; ambition as compulsion.
- **Shift**: Confesses and breaks with the dynasty.
- **Anchor relationships**: Corin (ethics ally), I-07 (creation), dynasty (power conflict).

### Corin Vale (Power-Level Investigator)
- **Core**: Applied Ethics lead; follows financial discrepancies to the truth.
- **Want / Need**: Keep the institution honest / accept institutional guilt.
- **Key tension**: Believes in reform until the evidence breaks that belief.
- **Shift**: Becomes Elara’s ally; helps leak sealed records.
- **Anchor relationships**: Elara (alliance), Finance Auditor (trail source).

## Supporting Cast (Use as System Embodiments)
- **Riva Olen**: Independent media organizer; Sol’s economic lifeline; conduit for public truth.
- **Finance Auditor**: Flags budget anomalies; triggers Corin’s investigation.
- **Security/Disposal Ops Lead**: Willfully ignorant middle manager; signed disposal orders.
- **Fixer/Scrapper/Tunnel Guide**: Wanderer who sells access; knowledge is a price.
- **Lattice Engineer/Tech Priest**: Maintains infrastructure; help is precise, reluctant, irreversible.

## Relationship Spine (Keep in Mind)
- **Sol ↔ Zara**: Perception vs politics; all-self meets all-world.
- **Sol ↔ Null**: Sol’s worldview breaks against another’s inner life.
- **Zara ↔ Null**: Political symbol vs person; fear of instrumentalizing him.
- **I-07 → Null → Echo**: Distress signal, answered; fusion yields something new.
- **Elara ↔ I-07**: Creator and consequence.
- **Elara ↔ Corin**: R&D vs Ethics; alliance after evidence converges.

## Thematic Mirrors (For Tone)
- Sol (all self, no world) ↔ Zara (all world, no self)
- Null (self through dissolution) ↔ I-07 (relief from unwanted existence)
- Elara (creator who can’t stop) ↔ I-07 (creation that can’t end)

## Agent Guidance
- Favor clear wants/needs, contradictions, and observable behavior.
- Keep character notes short and scene-ready.
- If adding characters, tie them to institutions or system pressures.
- Echo is not Null or I-07; it is continuity given mobility.

## Open Questions (Optional Prompts)
- Is Sol’s change genuine or a new kind of solipsism?
- Can Zara return from the violence line?
- What survives of Null inside Echo?
- Is Elara’s epilogue penance or compulsion in a softer form?
