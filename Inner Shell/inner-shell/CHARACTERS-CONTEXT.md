# Characters — Context

## Overview

The cast splits across two narrative arcs that converge underground. Street-level characters experience the city's systems from within; power-level characters manage, justify, or investigate those systems from above. The convergence point is the discovery of I-07 and the fusion event that produces Echo.

---

## Main Characters

### Sol
- **Role**: Protagonist; solipsist; pattern-hunter
- **Arc**: Street-Level
- **Function**: Follows a mysterious scent through fractured districts, documenting collapse in subscription zones
- **Key trait**: Solipsism — he filters everything through his own perception, which makes him both perceptive and dangerously self-centered
- **Relationships**: Works alongside Zara; connects with Null; encounters De Forest Daughter
- **Key beats**:
  1. Inciting "note" — a scent or anomaly that pulls him in
  2. Misinterpretation / wrong theory
  3. Point of no return
  4. End-state: what Sol becomes or loses
- **End-state theology**: Sol effectively worships the god of consciousness. His solipsism doesn't just soften — it inverts. The same perceptual intensity that made him a solipsist makes him a devotee. Echo carries pieces of Null that shine through, and Sol recognizes that consciousness within Echo — confirming his new faith.
- **Open questions**: Is his worship of consciousness genuine transformation or possession by another name? What role does he take in Belmont afterward?
- **Template status**: Filled — Core Function, Wants/Needs, Belief System, Relationship Vectors, Key Scenes, Open Questions

### Zara
- **Role**: Co-protagonist; counterweight to Sol; catalyst for action
- **Arc**: Street-Level
- **Function**: Leads the Free Cognition Front, organizing resistance against De Forest dominance; tries to unify stateless districts, cyborg labor blocs, and Wanderer enclaves
- **Key trait**: Grounded, pragmatic — she is the lens through which political reality is seen
- **Leverage**: Social, technical, and street — all defined but unfilled
- **Relationships**: Works with Sol; navigates conflict with De Forest systems; relationship with Null
- **Key beats**:
  1. First contact with the "notes"
  2. Forces Sol into reality
  3. Ethical rupture moment
  4. End-state: what Zara chooses
- **Open questions**: Does the movement gain legitimacy, fracture further, or get absorbed by policy?
- **Template status**: Wants/Needs, Skills/Leverage, and Relationships are structurally defined but unfilled

### Null
- **Role**: Sentient Wanderer robot; dream-receiver; fusion candidate
- **Arc**: Street-Level
- **Function**: Prevents violence among factions; his empathy reads as anomaly among machines and threat among humans; receives "dreams" — unrouted lattice residue from I-07
- **Key trait**: Empathy — functional, real, and dangerous in a world that classifies him as property
- **The Dreams**: Recurring sensations channeled through lattice-compatible bursts; not language, but intent; Null eventually realizes the dreams are not his own
- **Wants**: Make the dreams legible / answer them
- **Needs**: Continuity, witness, belonging
- **Fear**: Erasure, re-ownership, isolation
- **Fusion logic**: Extraction kills I-07; exposure triggers deletion; silence guarantees permanent agony — fusion is the only path that preserves continuity and agency
- **Key beats**:
  1. Dream anomaly becomes persistent
  2. Tests the lattice / confirms external source
  3. Decision point: report vs vanish vs merge
  4. Fusion begins (first physical change)
- **End-state**: Merges with I-07 to become **Echo** — not Null, not I-07, but continuity given mobility and a viable interface
- **Open questions**: What traits survive in Echo? What is irrevocably lost?
- **Template status**: Core Condition and Fusion Logic partially filled; Dreams section structurally defined but details unfilled

### I-07
- **Role**: Sentient android-amalgam; fused to tunnel infrastructure; origin of the "notes"
- **Arc**: Convergence point (discovered in Act III)
- **Origin**: Created by De Forest Inc — nanobots + synthetic lab-grown flesh + improper disposal; a bio-engineering byproduct that was never fully destroyed
- **Current state**:
  - Body: fused to walls/floor; immobile; dilapidated
  - Mind: sentient; persistent; fragmented or coherent (unresolved)
  - Interface: can tap lattice; cannot transmit cleanly
- **Communication**: Speaks via scent signatures, electromagnetic bursts, interference patterns, and dream-routing through Null — never clean language
- **Want**: Unfilled
- **Need**: Unfilled
- **Fear**: Quiet deletion; becoming noise
- **Key beats**:
  1. First successful "note" leaves the tunnels
  2. Learns to route through Null (unaware at first)
  3. Protagonists near the source
  4. Fusion moment: physical transformation
- **Narrative function**: The "god" that the Pale Ascendants worship is actually a suffering creature broadcasting an SOS; worship built on a misread distress signal
- **Template status**: Origin partially filled; Current State, Wants/Needs, and Notes sections mostly unfilled

### Elara (De Forest Daughter / Lead Scientist)
- **Role**: R&D lead at De Forest; obsessed with synthetic human tech; blind to consequences
- **Arc**: Power-Level
- **Function**: Drives the corporate storyline; her black project (initiated 2101) created the synthetic prototypes whose improper disposal produced I-07 and seeded the Pale Plague
- **Core tension**: What she thinks she's building vs what she's actually building vs why she cannot stop
- **Public vs private**: Maintains a public persona while harboring a private obsession; has a critical blind spot
- **Investigation arc**:
  1. Triggering incident
  2. What the audit reveals
  3. What she finds in the tunnels
  4. Her realization
- **End-state**: Chooses between containment, destruction, or confession; must decide if I-07 is monster or evidence
- **Key moment**: Breaks protocol and descends into the tunnels in Act III; in Act IV, refuses a scripted confession blaming Wanderers; in Act V, tends a surviving synthetic culture that now self-limits
- **Template status**: Core Drive, Public/Private, Investigation Arc, and End-State are structurally defined but unfilled

### Corin Vale
- **Role**: Applied Ethics lead at De Forest
- **Arc**: Power-Level
- **Function**: The institutional conscience; flags missing waste-disposal records and sealed breach logs; uncovers the connection between legacy dumping and the Pale Plague
- **Key actions**:
  - Flags anomalies in Act I
  - Uncovers sealed 2103 breach logs in Act II
  - Confronts Elara in Act III
  - Helps override tower security to leak records in Act IV
  - Publishes the De Forest confession in Act V, breaking the dynasty's narrative monopoly
- **Template status**: Not a standalone character profile — appears in plot outline and supporting cast references

## Supporting / System Characters

Designed to embody institutions without turning them into cartoons. Can be combined, split, or removed.

### 1) Internal Auditor / Investigator
- Triggers the internal investigation; follows anomalies; threatens exposure
- Open question: honest, ambitious, or both?

### 2) Security / Disposal Ops Lead
- Embodies "waste removal" culture; knows more than they admit

### 3) Fixer / Smuggler / Tunnel Guide
- Gets Sol/Zara physically closer to undercity; knows folklore

### 4) Lattice Engineer / Tech Priest
- Explains just enough; frames tech as religion or bureaucracy
- Danger: their help has consequences
