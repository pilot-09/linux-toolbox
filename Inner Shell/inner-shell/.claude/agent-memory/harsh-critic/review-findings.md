# Detailed Review Findings - 2026-02-14

## Systemic Issues (apply to all characters)

### 1. Voice Absence
Every character described in same authorial register. No dialogue samples, no speech patterns, no diction fingerprints. In multi-POV novel this is fatal -- reader cannot distinguish characters without chapter headings.

### 2. Self-Awareness Epidemic
All characters correctly identify their own central flaw. Sol knows his solipsism is defense. Zara knows she disappeared into the cause. Elara knows she is an addict. When characters are this self-aware, the reader has nothing to discover. Characters should be wrong about themselves.

### 3. Passivity Pattern
Sol perceives but never acts. Null follows but never resists. I-07 broadcasts but never chooses. Even Zara's big choices (violence line) are framed as reactions to external pressure. Need more characters making costly choices that drive plot forward.

### 4. Emotional Monotone
Every character processes experience through "thoughtful suffering." No humor, no pettiness, no cruelty, no banality. Novel risks reading as a tone poem.

### 5. Convergence Risk
All arcs terminate in same tunnel discovery scene. If that scene fails, nothing has its own ending. At least one character (Zara or Elara) needs a climactic moment independent of the tunnel.

## Character-Specific Priorities

### Sol - Needs Most Work
- Add active choices with plot consequences
- Add concrete stakes (what does he lose if he fails?)
- Add voice samples
- Make solipsism concretely harmful, not just philosophically limiting

### Zara - Strongest Foundation
- Add private interiority (habits, pleasures, memories outside the cause)
- Resolve whether violence rupture or identity question is primary arc
- Add voice samples (public rhetoric vs private speech)

### Null - Needs Resistance
- Add at least one moment of genuine doubt about fusion
- Add concrete relationships that make departure costly
- Show empathy overload texturally, not descriptively

### Elara - Needs Connection
- Connect to protagonists before Act III (even indirectly)
- Show the addiction, not just label it
- Make confession driven by compartment collapse, not guilt

### Riva Olen - Not Yet a Character
- Either develop fully or merge function into existing character

### Corin Vale - Needs Personal Stakes
- Why does Applied Ethics matter to him personally?
- What does leaving De Forest cost him beyond career?
